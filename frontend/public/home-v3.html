About Axis

Built in Wilmington, by someone you can actually reach.

I'm Ryan Lancellotti, a student at UNCW, and I built Axis. It started with a summer helping my friend's uncle on his roofing jobs. Most days meant scaling a ladder with a tape measure in the blistering Pennsylvania summer heat, measuring every slope, ridge and valley by hand, and then doing it all over again at the next house.

The measuring was only the start. Those same numbers then got typed into an estimate, copied onto a material order, called in to a supplier and passed along to the crew. Every handoff was another chance for a number to go wrong. There had to be a better way.

Axis is the tool I wish he'd had. It's designed, built and supported here in Wilmington. If a report looks wrong, you're writing to the person who wrote the code that made it, and I'd rather hear about it than have you guess.
